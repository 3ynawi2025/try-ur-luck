# Privacy Policy — Try Your Luck (جرب حظك)

**Last updated:** August 2025

Welcome to Try Your Luck (جرب حظك). Your privacy matters to us, and we are committed to protecting your data and being fully transparent about how we handle it. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

## 1. What information do we collect?

When you create an account or use the app, we may collect:

- **Email address (optional)** — to sign in, secure, and recover your account.
- **Username** — a unique identifier shown to you inside the game.
- **Display name** — the name other players see at tables and in majlis rooms.
- **Device ID** — used for account security, fraud prevention, and preventing multiple accounts.

We do **not** collect your phone number, your location, or payment information (there are no real-money payments in the game).

## 2. Voice chat (Agora)

Voice chat at tables and in majlis rooms runs through **Agora** and is **peer-to-peer (P2P)** directly between callers. **We do not record, store, or listen to voice conversations.**

## 3. How do we use your information?

We use your data only to:

- Operate your account and sign you in.
- Protect your account from fraud and unauthorized use.
- Show your name and username inside the game.
- Improve the experience and fix bugs.

## 4. Do we share or sell your data?

**We do not sell your data to any third party, we do not use it for ad tracking, and we do not show third-party ads.**

The processors we work with (such as **Supabase** for storage and **Agora** for voice) use data only to provide the service, under data-protection commitments.

## 5. Where is data stored?

Your account data is stored on the **Supabase** cloud platform with standard security measures (encryption in transit). **We do not store any voice data at all.**

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may request **deletion of your account and all your data** at any time by contacting us through the support page, and we will delete it within a reasonable timeframe.

## 7. Children

The app is rated **18+** and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor created an account, we will delete it.

## 8. Rules of Conduct & Abuse

We work to keep Try Your Luck a respectful and safe environment for everyone, so inside the app we provide:

- A **report flag ⚑** next to every player, with specific reasons: voice abuse, harassment, profanity, cheating, or disruption.
- A **block list** that lets you stop any user from interacting with you.
- Published **Rules of Conduct** explaining acceptable and unacceptable behavior.

When a violation is confirmed, the team applies penalties up to **mute** or **ban**, and offending content may be removed immediately. You can contact us for any report or appeal via the support page.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will publish the updated version at the same link with the update date, and your continued use constitutes acceptance of the new version.
